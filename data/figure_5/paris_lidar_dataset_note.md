# Paris Doppler Wind Lidar Dataset Note

- Title: Harmonised boundary layer wind profile dataset from six ground-based doppler wind lidars in a transect across Paris (France) from 2022-06-10 to 2024-03-05
- DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.17710093
- DOI URL: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.17710093
- License: cc-by-4.0
- Publication date: 2025-11-25
- Public-use status: yes; the mirrored Zenodo metadata declares a public DOI record under CC-BY-4.0.
- Time coverage in manuscript use: 2022-06-10 to 2024-03-05.
- Cadences mirrored locally: 600 s and 3600 s monthly tar archives.
- Structure used in this project: station metadata plus pairwise lower-boundary-layer summaries at 100-300 m and 200-500 m, event-placebo summaries, and availability tables.
- Description snippet:
  Quality controlled and harmonized dataset on wind direction and wind speed profiles from ground-based Doppler Wind LIDARS (DWL), deployed in a multi-institutional effort along a 40 km transect through the centre of the Paris Metropolitan area (France) for the period 2022-06-10 to 2024-03-05. The dataset, its derivation, quality control and evaluation against radiosondes and in-situ observations are decribed in the following manuscript: Morrison, W., Looschelders, D., C&eacute;spedes, J., Claxton, B., Drouin, M.-A., Dupont, J.-C., Faucheux, A., Haeffelin, M., Holst, C.
